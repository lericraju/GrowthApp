📱 Mobile PWA Access Guide

                
            

            

                
GrowthApp is a fully functional Progressive Web App (PWA) with background sync capabilities. Access and install it on your mobile device:

                
                
1. Deploy Your App

                
    
                    
  • Push your repository files to GitHub.
    • 
                    
  • Connect the repo to a free hosting provider like Vercel or Netlify.
    • 
                    
  • Your dashboard will be instantly live over a secure HTTPS endpoint.
    • 
                


                
2. Install on Mobile Home Screen

                
    
                    
  • iOS Safari: Navigate to your URL, tap the Share button, and choose Add to Home Screen.
    • 
                    
  • Android Chrome: Navigate to your URL, click the Menu (3 dots), and click Install App.
    • 
                    
  • The app will run full-screen, bypassing Safari/Chrome browser frames.
    • 
                


                
3. Enable Cross-Device Live Sync

                
    
                    
  • Set up a free project on Supabase.
    • 
                    
  • Go to the Database & Sync tab inside this app, input your URL and Anon Key, and click save.
    • 
                    
  • Input the exact same credentials on both your phone and computer. Your logs, checkbox checkmarks, and physique charts will live sync in real-time across both screens!
    •
